# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Zachary Roch, George Atia, and Yue Wang for developing and
sharing the work associated with *Model-Free Robust Average-Reward
Reinforcement Learning with Sample Complexity Analysis*. The paper's precise
claim structure and the surrounding implementation artifacts make it possible
to inspect the algorithm, connect each claim to an evidence producer, and keep
the reproduction record honest about what has and has not been reproduced.

This repository is an organizational record of the source workspace. It does
not imply author endorsement of the results recorded here.
